# Clinical Evaluation Report (CER) - Clinica-LENS

## 1. General Details
- **Device Name:** Clinica-LENS (Longitudinal Explainable Network System)
- **Version:** Ultra-Enterprise 1.0
- **Manufacturer:** Clinica AI
- **Date of Report:** 2026-05-13

## 2. Intended Purpose
Clinica-LENS is intended to assist qualified radiologists in the detection and characterization of lung pathologies in adult patients using multimodal inputs (Chest X-ray and Clinical Notes). It provides spatial attribution heatmaps and longitudinal progression analysis to support clinical decision-making.

## 3. Clinical Background
Chest X-ray interpretation is subject to intra- and inter-observer variability. AI-based computer-aided detection (CADe) and computer-aided diagnosis (CADx) tools have been shown to improve sensitivity and specificity in clinical workflows.

## 4. State of the Art
Evaluation of current literature regarding DenseNet architectures (CheXNet) and Transformer-based fusion for medical imaging.

## 5. Clinical Data Analysis
- **Pivotal Study Design:** Multicenter retrospective study.
- **Reference Standard:** Consensus reading by three board-certified radiologists.
- **Performance Metrics:** AUC-ROC, Sensitivity, Specificity, and Calibration (Platt Scaling).

## 6. Conclusion
The clinical data supports the safety and performance of the Clinica-LENS system for its intended use.
